# Supply Agreement — Mirefield Packaging GmbH

**Parties:** Brightloom Inc. ("Buyer") and Mirefield Packaging GmbH ("Seller")
**Effective date:** 1 June 2024
**Goods:** Custom corrugated boxes and protective inserts, per purchase orders issued under this Agreement.

## 1. Term
1.1 This Agreement runs for three (3) years from the Effective Date and does not renew automatically.

## 2. Volume commitment
2.1 Buyer commits to purchase a minimum of 250,000 units per contract year.
2.2 If Buyer purchases fewer units, Buyer pays a shortfall fee of EUR 0.40 per unit below the minimum.

## 3. Termination
3.1 Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within forty-five (45) days.
3.2 Buyer may not terminate for convenience.

## 4. Liability
4.1 Seller's liability for defective goods is limited to replacing the goods or refunding their price.
4.2 Seller's total liability under this Agreement is capped at EUR 500,000.

## 5. Change of control
5.1 Either party undergoing a change of control must notify the other party within ten (10) business days. The other party may then terminate on ninety (90) days' notice without penalty.

## 6. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Germany. The courts of Hamburg have exclusive jurisdiction.
