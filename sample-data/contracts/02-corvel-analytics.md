# Software Subscription Agreement — Corvel Analytics, Inc.

**Parties:** Brightloom Inc. ("Customer") and Corvel Analytics, Inc. ("Vendor")
**Effective date:** 15 March 2025
**Services:** Subscription to the Corvel demand-forecasting platform for up to 40 named users.

## 1. Term
1.1 The subscription term is one (1) year and renews automatically for one-year terms unless Customer cancels at least thirty (30) days before renewal.

## 2. Fees and price changes
2.1 Annual fees are payable in advance.
2.2 Vendor may change its fees at renewal on sixty (60) days' notice. There is no cap on the increase.

## 3. Data
3.1 Customer data remains Customer's property. Vendor may use aggregated, de-identified usage data to improve its products.
3.2 On termination, Vendor deletes Customer data within thirty (30) days unless Customer requests an export first.

## 4. Liability
4.1 Vendor's total liability is limited to the fees paid in the three (3) months before the claim.
4.2 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

## 5. Change of control
5.1 If Vendor undergoes a change of control, Customer may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' notice and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees.

## 6. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.
