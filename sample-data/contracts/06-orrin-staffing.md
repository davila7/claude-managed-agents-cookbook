# Staffing Services Agreement — Orrin Staffing Partners

**Parties:** Brightloom Inc. ("Client") and Orrin Staffing Partners ("Agency")
**Effective date:** 1 April 2026
**Services:** Temporary warehouse staff supplied to Client's distribution centers during peak seasons.

## 1. Term and renewal
1.1 The initial term is eighteen (18) months. It renews automatically for one-year terms unless either party gives sixty (60) days' notice of non-renewal.

## 2. Fees
2.1 Client pays the hourly bill rates in Schedule 1. Overtime is billed at 1.5 times the bill rate.
2.2 If Client hires an Agency worker directly within six (6) months of the worker's last assignment, Client pays a conversion fee of 20% of the worker's first-year salary.

## 3. Termination
3.1 Either party may terminate for convenience on thirty (30) days' notice.

## 4. Liability and indemnity
4.1 Agency indemnifies Client against wage-and-hour claims by Agency workers.
4.2 Each party's liability is otherwise capped at USD 1,000,000.

## 5. Change of control
5.1 Agency must notify Client of any change of control within thirty (30) days. Client may terminate on fifteen (15) days' notice following that notice.

## 6. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
