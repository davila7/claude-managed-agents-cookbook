# Cloud Hosting Agreement — Quillon Cloud Services LLC

**Parties:** Brightloom Inc. ("Customer") and Quillon Cloud Services LLC ("Provider")
**Effective date:** 1 January 2025
**Services:** Managed hosting for Customer's order-management system, with a 99.9% monthly uptime commitment.

## 1. Term and renewal
1.1 The initial term is three (3) years. It renews automatically for successive two-year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 2. Service levels
2.1 If monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, Customer receives a service credit of 5% of that month's fees for each full 0.1% below the commitment, up to 30% of the monthly fee.
2.2 Service credits are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for downtime.

## 3. Termination
3.1 Customer may terminate for convenience only by paying all fees due for the remainder of the then-current term.

## 4. Liability
4.1 Provider's total liability is capped at the fees paid in the six (6) months preceding the claim.
4.2 The cap does not apply to Provider's data-protection obligations.

## 5. Assignment and change of control
5.1 Provider may assign this Agreement to an affiliate or to an acquirer of its business without Customer's consent.

## 6. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
