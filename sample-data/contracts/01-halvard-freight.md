# Master Services Agreement — Halvard Freight Ltd.

**Parties:** Brightloom Inc. ("Customer") and Halvard Freight Ltd. ("Supplier")
**Effective date:** 1 February 2025
**Services:** Inbound and outbound freight handling for Customer's two distribution centers.

## 1. Term and renewal
1.1 The initial term is two (2) years from the Effective Date.
1.2 This Agreement renews automatically for successive one-year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 2. Fees
2.1 Customer pays the rates in Schedule A, invoiced monthly, net 45 days.
2.2 Supplier may increase rates once per year by no more than the change in the US Consumer Price Index.

## 3. Termination
3.1 Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice.
3.2 Customer may terminate for convenience on sixty (60) days' written notice, paying a termination fee equal to two months of average fees.

## 4. Liability
4.1 Each party's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
4.2 The cap does not apply to breaches of confidentiality or to a party's indemnification obligations.

## 5. Assignment
5.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of substantially all assets.

## 6. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
