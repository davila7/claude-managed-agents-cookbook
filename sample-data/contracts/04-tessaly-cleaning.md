# Facility Services Agreement — Tessaly Cleaning Co.

**Parties:** Brightloom Inc. ("Client") and Tessaly Cleaning Co. ("Contractor")
**Effective date:** 1 September 2025
**Services:** Nightly cleaning of Client's headquarters office, five nights per week.

## 1. Term and renewal
1.1 The initial term is one (1) year. This Agreement then continues month to month until either party terminates it.

## 2. Fees
2.1 Client pays a fixed monthly fee of USD 6,800, invoiced in arrears, net 30 days.

## 3. Termination
3.1 Either party may terminate at any time on thirty (30) days' written notice, without penalty.

## 4. Insurance and liability
4.1 Contractor maintains general liability insurance of at least USD 2,000,000 per occurrence.
4.2 Contractor is liable for damage to Client property caused by its personnel, without a cap.

## 5. Personnel
5.1 Contractor runs background checks on all personnel assigned to Client's premises.

## 6. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.
